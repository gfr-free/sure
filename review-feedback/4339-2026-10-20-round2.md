**🎯 Functional Correctness** | **🟡 Minor** | **⚡ Quick win**

**Normalize dashes and whitespace in `mask_identifier`, so masked values match across views.**

`mask_identifier` calls `last4` without `strip_dashes` or `strip_whitespace`. The same Akahu `other_account` value therefore gets two different masks:

- Transaction notes (`AkahuEntry::Processor#notes`, Line 131) strip dashes and show `•••2300`.
- Additional details (`TransactionsHelper#build_transaction_extra_details`) show `•••3-00`.

The comment in `AkahuEntry::Processor#notes` gives the reason for stripping dashes: without it, the mask shows "little more than the suffix". `mask_identifier` does not follow that rule. A user cannot match the counterparty in the notes with the counterparty in the details panel.

IBANs have a similar problem. If a provider stores an IBAN in groups (`"DE89 3704 ... 0178 90"`), the mask becomes `•••8 90`.

Apply both options to the account part and to the full value. Also update the assertion in `test/models/identifier_mask_test.rb` at Line 55 from `"•••3-00"` to `"•••2300"`.
