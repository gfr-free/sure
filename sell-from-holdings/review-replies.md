## Codex P2, app/models/account.rb (picker reads materialized holdings only) — leave open, reply only
This is intentional for now. The picker lists materialized holdings, so a purchase recorded seconds ago appears once its sync finishes (the picker reloads whenever the date changes). Correctness doesn't depend on it: the sale limit itself recalculates the position from trades, so it never relies on stale rows. Computing the picker from the forward calculator would run a full portfolio calculation on every form open, which seemed too costly for the short window. Happy to change it if maintainers prefer that trade-off.

## CodeRabbit, app/models/trade/sale_limit.rb (future-dated positions ignore announced splits) — resolve
Good catch, fixed in 0bb4913. The limit used to add future-dated trades to today's position in the same units and skipped splits with a future ex-date. A trade dated after an announced split is written in post-split units, so a reverse split could let a sale through that later left the position negative (and a forward split refused one that fit). Every quantity is now converted into units of the sale date on its own date, with past and announced splits alike. Two new tests (reverse and forward split ahead of a future-dated sale) fail on the previous commit and pass now.
